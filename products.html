QS-AMP · Post-Quantum Cryptography

QS-AMP — Quantum-Safe Assessment & Migration Platform

Discover. Assess. Benchmark. Migrate. Monitor.

QS-AMP helps organizations prepare for the post-quantum era. It discovers cryptographic assets, identifies quantum-vulnerable algorithms, generates Cryptographic Bills of Materials (CBOMs), assesses risk, benchmarks Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) algorithms, and builds a clear migration path to quantum-safe cryptography.

Widely used public-key algorithms such as RSA, ECC, and Diffie-Hellman are expected to be breakable by sufficiently capable quantum computers. Data encrypted today can be captured now and decrypted later, which makes early preparation a present-day security priority, not a future one.

QS-AMP turns post-quantum readiness from an open-ended research problem into a structured, measurable programme of work.

How QS-AMP Works

01 · Discover

Cryptographic asset discovery

Locate keys, certificates, protocols, libraries, and algorithms in use across applications and infrastructure.

02 · Assess

Quantum risk assessment

Flag quantum-vulnerable algorithms, generate CBOMs, and prioritise risk by data sensitivity and exposure.

03 · Benchmark

PQC algorithm benchmarking

Measure performance, key and signature sizes, and compatibility of PQC algorithms in your own environment.

04 · Migrate

Guided migration path

Plan phased, hybrid, and crypto-agile transitions to quantum-safe cryptography with minimal disruption.

05 · Monitor

Continuous monitoring

Track migration progress, detect new cryptographic usage, and keep the inventory current over time.

Key Capabilities

  • Automated cryptographic inventory and discovery
  • CBOM (Cryptographic Bill of Materials) generation
  • Identification of quantum-vulnerable algorithms and weak configurations
  • Risk scoring and migration prioritisation
  • Benchmarking of NIST-standardised PQC algorithms, including ML-KEM, ML-DSA, and SLH-DSA
  • Migration roadmaps with progress reporting
